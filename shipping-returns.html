Shipping and returns

Where we ship

We ship to Canada and the United States. Standard shipping is $15 CAD to Canada and $25 CAD to the United States, added at checkout. All prices are in Canadian dollars.

After you order

You will get a receipt by email straight away. When your set ships we will email you tracking details. If an address looks wrong, we will contact you before sending.

Duties and taxes

Orders to the United States may be subject to import duties or taxes charged by the carrier or customs on delivery. These are not included in the price you pay at checkout.

Returns

You can return a set within 14 days of receiving it, as long as it is unworn, unwashed and has its tags. Write to contact@shophinas.com with your order details and we will tell you where to send it. Return shipping is paid by you unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.

Refunds

Once we receive and check your return, we refund the original payment method. Your bank may take several business days to show it.

Damaged or wrong item

If something is not right, write to contact@shophinas.com within 7 days of delivery with a photo and we will make it right.

Sizing

Sets are sold in S, M and L, and the sweatshirt and pants are sold together as one set. If you are unsure of your size, write to contact@shophinas.com before ordering.
