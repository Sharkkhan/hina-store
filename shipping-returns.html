Returns

Unworn sets in their original condition can be returned within 14 days of delivery.

  • Sets must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition with tags attached.
  • To start a return, write to us through the contact page with your order number.
  • Refunds are issued to your original payment method once we receive and inspect the return.
  • Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.
