When you pay, Stripe collects your name, email, phone number, shipping address and card details on its own secure page. We receive your name, email, phone, shipping address and what you ordered. We never see or store your full card number.

Fulfilling and shipping your order, sending receipts and tracking, answering your questions, and handling returns. We do not sell your information.
